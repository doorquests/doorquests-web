## Premise

With the Sevrin River temporarily dry, archaeologist Dr. Mara Senn has only hours to investigate a newly exposed tomb before the floodwater returns. The burial chamber holds valuable relics, but its ancient river measurements may matter more than gold. Mara must weigh the excavation, evidence for a dangerous flood cycle, and the safety of Ondo.

## Creative identity

Claude's independent expedition design features a live tomb cross-section showing the advancing flood, a site clock, bag slots, a numbered finds register, and handwritten field notes. Fonts are Big Shoulders Display, Faustina, Kalam, and IBM Plex Mono; the palette draws on lapis, silt, ochre, stone, and river blue.

## Interactive mechanics

- Three numbered choices at decision points, with time-sensitive consequences.
- Flood countdown begins at 13:00, initially showing four hours and thirty minutes.
- The dam radio can revise the expected flood arrival time.
- Five documented finds, seven field notes, and evidence from the Measuring Stair.
- Choice between preserving the mechanism and allowing destructive access.
- Evidence collection and what Mara carries affect the ending.
- Browser-local saved progress, back, restart, persistent ending discovery, and collapsible side records.
