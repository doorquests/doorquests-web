## Premise

Wynn Abernethy receives a commission from Prince Thistlewick for slippers to wear at the Midwinter Revel. The written requirements ask for silent soles, uppers that recall June, warm lining, and a gift sewn inside. One crossed-out word points toward the Prince's real need: courage rather than grandeur.

## Presentation

A changing SVG slipper illustrates the four materials chosen at the workbench. The interface shows the Prince's commission, daylight remaining, the basket, and lore discovered along the way. The typography uses Fondamento, Vollkorn, and Nunito, with a fairy-tale palette of moss, plum, and honey.

## Choices and mechanics

- Three numbered decisions at each scene, plus back and restart.
- Three gathering visits before dusk, with the Old Well, Widow Brannagh, beekeeper's meadow, and village inn as possible stops.
- Four crafted components: sole, upper, lining, and charm.
- Material properties affect the dance, the Prince's response, and the ending.
- Five discoverable lore entries, including Gran's advice and the Prince's anxiety.
- Browser-local progress restoration and previously discovered endings.
- Collapsible basket and lore display on narrow screens.
