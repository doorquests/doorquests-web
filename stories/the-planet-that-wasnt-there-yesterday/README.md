# The Planet That Wasn't There Yesterday

**The Axiom Continuum · Daily Door #12**  
**Genre:** First-contact science-fiction mystery / interactive diplomacy

## Premise

At 04:12 UTC, an inhabited planet appears sixty degrees ahead of Earth. Everyone on Earth remembers that point being empty; everyone on the other world, Ammen, says Earth has only just appeared. Astronomer and newly appointed liaison Dr. Sunita Rao communicates with archivist Tesh Orrin while military leaders on both worlds consider escalation. Ancient traces in star charts, lullabies, and lunar structures point toward a shared, deliberately forgotten history.

## Creative identity

The experience retains its own split-sky visual design: paired Earth/Ammen canvas scenes and a signal seam, contrasting blue and teal transmissions, a trust indicator, a timed veil countdown, and a side-by-side evidence ledger. Typography uses Syne, Lora, and Martian Mono. This design is distinct from the other TAC Daily Doors.

## Interactive mechanics

- Three numbered decisions per non-ending scene, with Earth-side, Ammen-side, shared, or risky option labels.
- Trust between worlds changes with diplomatic decisions and determines critical outcomes.
- Three paired memories to reconcile: star charts, lullabies, and lunar Loom structures.
- An in-story clock and a sixty-one-hour veil deadline after the Loom inscription is uncovered.
- Major contacts include Tesh Orrin, General Ines Harrow, and Speaker Corvel Vaal.
- Six endings, browser-local saved progress and restoration, retrace/restart, and persistent ending tracking.
- Responsive, collapsible evidence and contact details, with reduced-motion animation support.
