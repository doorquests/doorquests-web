**Genre:** Diplomatic mystery / interactive historical-style political drama

**Date:** 2026-10-15

## Premise

At a summit intended to prevent war over the island of Sel, twenty-four-year-old interpreter Teodora Lisk must carry the words of two rulers who do not speak each other's language. Her teacher, Master Benedek, recently died after falling from the abbey's bell tower. As King Mattias and Doge Lucia negotiate, Teodora discovers idioms whose literal translations could provoke conflict, evidence of a forged letter, and political pressure to distort what is said. Each choice tests the boundary between communicating meaning and inventing it.

## Creative identity

Claude's original diplomatic summit design is preserved: two opposing crowns flank an animated balance scale, while the room's mood and the interpreter's suspicion meter change with the dialogue. Spoken-language cards distinguish original wording, word-for-word glosses, and the player's chosen rendering. The palette uses Ostravan blue, Hesperine ochre, and interpreter green, with Marcellus, Cardo, and Sora typography.

## Interactive mechanics

- Three numbered choices per decision point, with translation strategies labelled **Literal**, **The meaning**, or **Alter it**.
- Room tension and suspicion respond to choices; excessive suspicion may end the summit early.
- Seven discoverable glossary clues, including two potentially dangerous idioms, the unusual written form *thou*, evidence from Benedek's room, and political motives.
- Limited conversations during the recess, followed by confrontation over a suspicious letter.
- A record of the day's translations captures the approach taken at each exchange.
- Six endings, browser-local progress restoration, back/restart, and persistent ending discoveries.
- Responsive, collapsible glossary and translation record, with reduced-motion support.
