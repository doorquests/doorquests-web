# Gravity Is Optional Today

**The Axiom Continuum · Daily Door #4**  
**Genre:** Whimsical speculative adventure  
**Setting:** Little Plumbley on its centennial Optional Day

## Story premise

A town mail carrier follows an extraordinary mail route as gravity becomes adjustable, uncovers Ada Plumbley’s message, and faces a deciding sunset vote.

## Individual visual identity

Playful illustrated skyline, floating objects, handwritten-style display typography, and pastel-to-midnight colors.

## Playable features

Interactive gravity dial and animated sky; branching town encounters; a mailbag containing seven discoveries; simulated time and sunset countdown; six endings; back, restart, browser-local saves and ending discovery.

## Endings (6)

1. Feet on the Ground
2. The Great Duck Pond Landing
3. Above It All
4. The Kite Town
5. Gravity Day
6. Return to Sender
