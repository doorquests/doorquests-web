## Premise

At 04:12, the North Sea withdraws beyond the horizon, exposing a vast seabed and an impossible wall of standing water. Rhona Gale, a Saltby lifeboat mechanic, sets out on foot to find her sister Kit, whose research ship *Meridian* went silent near mysterious pillars 35 kilometres offshore. Old coastal warnings and scientific evidence suggest that the sea will return at the next dawn. Rhona must balance travel time, drinking water, companions, and the possibility of warning everyone still exploring the exposed seabed.

## Creative identity

The original design presents an animated bathymetric side profile spanning the coast, wrecks, Ravenser, the cordon, Doggerland, and the Tideworks, with the water wall and Rhona's position visualized. Status instruments show the clock, distance from shore, estimated return of the sea, and water supplies. Discovery entries appear in a field log alongside the current party. Typography combines Saira Extra Condensed, Merriweather, and Red Hat Mono; the palette contrasts seawater blue with urgent orange.

## Interactive mechanics

- Three numbered choices per scene, with back and restart navigation.
- A journey across the exposed seabed, with travel times, distance markers, and limited drinking water.
- Optional encounters with Old Ezra, a stranded seal pup, Ollie and his dog Pilot, and Lieutenant Imogen Pryce.
- Seven possible field-log discoveries, including the Ravenser bell, Doggerland stone, repaired-radio parts, and Kit's Tideworks readings.
- The route to the most positive outcome depends on both obtaining working radio components and learning the predicted return time.
- Six endings reflecting survival, rescue, public warning, early retreat, and the mystery beyond the wall.
- Browser-local saved progress, restored sessions, persistent ending collection, mobile-friendly collapsible notes, and reduced-motion support.
