## Premise

Karl Brandt, a railway switchman working an overnight shift, receives a tobacco tin from an injured stranger. Its film reveals twelve undercover names. The first, ROOK, services the very drop Karl has been instructed to use. With midnight approaching, Karl chooses between obedience, caution, contacting the West, and protecting his sister Lotte across the Berlin Wall.

## Presentation

Claude's self-contained signal-box design uses a live track diagram, numbered railway levers, a film-strip display, amber/red signal lights, and an in-story clock. Fonts: Antonio, PT Serif, and Cutive Mono. Original green, red, steel and amber palette retained.

## Mechanics

- 25 authored scenes; six endings.
- Three numbered options per decision with state-dependent outcomes.
- Simulated night watch from 22:41 to midnight; a 23:50 goods train.
- Suspicion indicator and seven discoverable details.
- Twelve-frame film display and alternative telephone contact.
- Local saves, back, restart, and persistent ending discoveries.
- Collapsible mobile dossier.
