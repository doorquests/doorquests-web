## Premise

After her mother's death, Nell returns to Ashcombe House, empty for thirty years after her brother Danny died in a flood. Suspended rain inside each room reveals fragments of the family's last night together. Nell must uncover what truly happened and decide how to carry that knowledge forward.

## Visual identity

Claude's atmospheric design preserves animated rain, lightning, a storm-window house illustration, lit room windows, an accumulating water meter, and a timeline of recovered memories. Fonts: Gloock, Gelasio, and IBM Plex Sans Condensed. Palette: rain-blue, slate, and lamplight-gold.

## Mechanics

- Explore rooms and release suspended storms to restore past events.
- Eight timeline moments from 9:14 p.m. through 11:52 p.m.
- Floodwater increases with exploration and may affect the ending.
- Key discoveries include Dad's apology, Danny unpacking, Nell's note, and Danny searching for her.
- Optional finds include a cassette tape and message by telephone.
- Three numbered choices, local progress saving, back/restart, and ending discovery.
- Collapsible timeline for smaller screens and motion preferences.
