## Story premise

A traveler who remembers that a king exists investigates a realm unable to remember its monarch. The Charter Engine, the archivist Sabeline Quill, and evidence of the Chancellor’s forged decrees frame choices about memory, authority, and law.

## Individual visual identity

Manuscript and royal archive: medieval typography, gilt and wax accents, seal imagery.

## Playable features

Branching investigation; seven clues in the Ledger of Things Remembered; evidence-dependent resolutions; back, restart, browser-local progress and ending discovery.

## Endings (6)

1. The King Returns
2. The Warm Throne
3. The Regent's Bow
4. The Quiet Road
5. The Second Forgetting
6. The Open Ledger
