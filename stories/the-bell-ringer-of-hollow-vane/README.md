# The Bell-Ringer of Hollow Vane

**The Axiom Continuum · Daily Door #5**  
**Genre:** Medieval suspense  
**Setting:** The walled town of Hollow Vane and the surrounding Thornwold

## Story premise

Wren, an apprentice bell-ringer, must interpret warnings about a supposed grain caravan, investigate the missing ringer Edric, protect the town’s flock, and decide when to sound the alarm.

## Individual visual identity

Dusk-gradient tower scene, animated bell, old-world lettering and bronze/ember accents.

## Playable features

Distinct bell peal codes; optional synthesized bell sound; town-heed/trust meter; evidence and seven discoveries; timing-sensitive choices; six endings; back, restart, browser-local saves and ending discovery.

## Endings (6)

1. The Gate Holds
2. Ring True
3. The Unheeded Bell
4. Open Gates
5. The Reeve's Choice
6. The Thornwold Keeps
