## Premise

Five minutes before curtain, understudy Pip Calloway is told to play Inspector Thorne because the leading actor is trapped in a stage lift with a lobster. The director, Ludovic Frane, keeps rewriting the play during the performance. Pip must balance the original murder mystery, increasingly absurd revisions, audience reaction, and the possibility that stage manager Dot knows more about the original script than anyone realizes.

## Creative identity

A self-contained, dark theatrical presentation with velvet curtains, a glowing marquee, brass trim, script-page inserts in revision colours, an applause dial, a plot-coherence gauge, and backstage gossip. Typography uses Limelight, EB Garamond, Courier Prime, and Josefin Sans. This identity is intentionally distinct from the other Daily Doors.

## Interactive mechanics

- Numbered stage choices: follow the script, use rewritten pages, or improvise.
- An applause meter, plot gauge, and changing genre record the performance's direction.
- Script-page revisions progress from WHITE through BLUE, PINK, YELLOW, GREEN, GOLDENROD, and CHERRY.
- Seven discoverable backstage facts; the interval permits at most two conversations.
- Conditional finale, including the route to Dot's original ending.
- Six endings, browser-local progress restoration, back/restart controls, and persistent ending discoveries.
- Collapsible backstage notes and revision list on smaller screens.
