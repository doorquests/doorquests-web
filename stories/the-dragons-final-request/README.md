# The Dragon's Final Request

**The Axiom Continuum · Daily Door #20**  
**Genre:** Epic fantasy / moral adventure / historical mystery  
**World and setting:** The Kingdom of Aldermere, during the final seven days of the Long Burning  
**Reading time:** Approximately 10–15 minutes per run (original design estimate)  
**Status:** COMPLETE — APPROVED (companion documentation; Founder play review separate)  
**Date:** 2026-10-15

> The last dragon will surrender if someone investigates the forgotten origin of the war.

## Premise

For three hundred years the Kingdom of Aldermere has been at war with dragons, a conflict the minstrels call **the Long Burning**. Only one dragon remains: **Ysmeral**, ancient and bronze-scaled, living on the black northern peak of **Cinderhorn**.

Ysmeral flies over the capital without burning it. Instead, she sears a demand into the castle gate:

> *I am Ysmeral, the last. I will lay my head at your King's feet, if one of you climbs Cinderhorn and tells me truly how this war began. You have seven days.*

King Edric orders the Royal Chronicler to tell her the accepted Legend of Highcairn. His elderly chronicler cannot travel, so the task falls to the apprentice, **Sefa Marlowe**, twenty-two years old, with ink on her fingers. Sefa must decide whether to deliver the official account, uncover the truth, or go beyond words to save what is left of dragonkind.

## How to play

- Each scene presents **three choices**. Select one or press **1**, **2** or **3**.
- **Retrace your last step** reverses the previous decision; **Start over** returns to the castle gate.
- Six distinct endings can be discovered. Completed endings are remembered locally in the browser.
- The game's map draws Sefa's traveled route as a dashed ember line, and seven day markers show the deadline. The eighth marker (⚔) represents the approaching royal army.
- Visiting a location costs days **only the first time**; optional delays consume time. The corrected published HTML sends the journey to **The Army Marches** when time enters **Day 8**, including travel to Cinderhorn.
- The map, histories, companions and egg tracking update as decisions are made.

## The Two Histories

The primary investigation consists of five discoveries. The sidebar presents the **Legend** in gold beside **the Truth**; once evidence is uncovered, the official account is struck through.

| Entry | Official Legend | Revealed Truth | Location |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The Beginning** | Morvhast burned Highcairn without cause; King Aldric declared war afterward. | The King declared war **three days before** Highcairn burned. | Royal Archive |
| **Highcairn** | Innocent city burned in a night attack. | Royal alchemists worked there; the dragons tried to reclaim their stolen young. | Highcairn |
| **The Hatchery** | Dragon breeding caves purged by heroes. | Royal cages held captured hatchlings for harvesting. | The Hatchery |
| **The Chronicle** | An accurate royal account, copied by monks. | Aldric ordered pages removed; his deathbed confession survived. | Grey Quill monastery |
| **Heartfire** | The King's physicians' secret restorative. | A draught made from dragon hearts that prolonged the royal line. | Royal Archive |

The most hopeful resolution requires **at least four truths** and rescuing the **last dragon egg** from the royal vault. Full discovery enriches the evidence and optional character scenes; the revised ending narration accounts for discoveries that players did not make.

## Characters

- **Sefa Marlowe:** Apprentice chronicler and playable protagonist.
- **Ysmeral:** Last dragon; asks for a truthful history, not merely a familiar story.
- **King Edric:** Ruler of Aldermere, kept alive by heartfire.
- **Sir Bartholomew Gant:** Veteran dragon fighter whose experience challenges the official Legend. Can become Sefa's ally after witnessing the Hatchery.
- **Princess Elin:** The King's fifteen-year-old granddaughter, who can reveal the egg and supply the vault key.
- **Old Mab:** Highcairn shepherd whose ballad preserves what happened.
- **Brother Anselm:** Blind, 102-year-old monk of the Grey Quill who guards the removed pages.
- **King Aldric:** Founder of the original war three centuries before; his confession reveals its purpose.

## All six endings

| ID | Ending | How it is reached |
|---|---|---|
| **E1** | **The Last Egg** · *truest* | Arrive by Day 7, uncover four or more truths, bring the egg, and reveal it while telling Ysmeral the truth. |
| **E2** | **She Lays Down Her Head** | Deliver sufficient truth without presenting the egg; Ysmeral honors her promise, at a terrible cost. |
| **E3** | **The Legend Holds** | Recite the official Legend as the King commanded. |
| **E4** | **Half a History** | Attempt to tell the truth after uncovering fewer than four discoveries. |
| **E5** | **The Army Marches** | Allow the journey to enter Day 8 before completing the mission. |
| **E6** | **Keeper of the Legend** | Accept King Edric's royal warrant to preserve the official story. |

At Cinderhorn, an additional choice can conceal the egg instead of presenting it; that choice does not earn the truest ending even if the egg is carried.

## Walkthrough to the truest ending — spoilers

This path uses all seven days and gathers all five truths:

1. **Day 1:** Optionally ask Sir Bartholomew what he knows; examine the **Royal Archive** to uncover *The Beginning* and *Heartfire*.
2. **Day 3:** Travel to **the Hatchery** (two days) to reveal the royal cages. With Gant's earlier conversation, he can change his understanding.
3. **Day 4:** Visit **Highcairn** (one day) to learn the ballad and its history.
4. **Day 5:** Visit **the Grey Quill** (one day), uncover Aldric's confession, and learn of the egg.
5. **Day 6:** Return to **the royal vault** (one day) and take the egg.
6. **Day 7:** Climb **Cinderhorn** (one day), tell Ysmeral the truth, and unwrap the egg.

A one-day audience with King Edric cannot be added to this exact route without exceeding the deadline.

## Design notes

- **Visual identity:** Muted green-grey map tones, ember-red truth and route accents, and gold for the official Legend. Fonts: *Almendra*, *Almendra SC*, and *Spectral*. Light and dark palettes.
- **Narrative artifacts:** Ysmeral's demand appears in a glowing, burned-stone panel. The Proclamations Roll, Old Mab's ballad and Aldric's confession are displayed as parchment-like leaves. Truth discoveries receive a distinct ✦ marker.
- **Map:** Inline SVG of the kingdom, rivers, burned country and Cinderhorn, showing Sefa's traveled route and the dragon's presence or absence.
- **Mobile and accessibility:** Single-column layout on smaller screens, collapsible reference panels, safe-area spacing, keyboard choices, back/restart, and reduced-motion accommodation.
- **Persistence:** Previously found endings are stored under `dragon-endings`. Current progress and history are also saved locally under `tac-dragon-final-request-daily-door-v1`.

## Reconciliation and validation

The uploaded `dragons-request.md` is a substantial original design reference, unlike several earlier Claude Markdown exports that contained only the opening interface. Its premise, cast, five-history matrix, places, signature presentation, ending names, spoiler walkthrough and Grand Saga possibilities are retained here.

Where the export and shipped version differ, the **committed HTML is canonical**. Specifically, the updated story enforces the Day 7 deadline as soon as a transition enters Day 8 and adjusts ending text to reflect what the player actually discovered. The Markdown's earlier statement that 200,000 states were tested refers to a previous testing claim; the **final reviewed HTML** instead passed JavaScript syntax validation and an **exhaustive audit of 4,279 reachable states and 7,575 transitions**, reaching all six endings without an invalid destination. Automated validation is not the same as a manual live-browser playthrough.
