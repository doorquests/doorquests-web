The Ship in the Bottle

Turn the bottle. Pull the right thread. Sail her home.

Input mode: Manual

How to play

  • Roll turns the bottle around its long axis in 15° steps (dial, ◀ ▶ buttons, or ←/→).
  • Pitch tips the neck up or down through five positions, −30° to +30° (lever, ▲ ▼ buttons, or ↑/↓).
  • Each thread row says whether it is TAUT or SLACK. Choose a taut thread, then press Pull. Its part rises and locks.
  • Some parts block others once raised (SNAGGED). Slacken last thread undoes your most recent pull, as many times as you like.
  • When every part is locked, Bring bottle upright.
  • Keyboard: with the bottle picture focused, arrows turn and tip, 1–6 choose a thread, Space or Enter pulls. Nothing is timed. Progress lives in this page only; reloading starts over.
  • Tilt is optional. The whole game works with touch, mouse or keyboard.

    The Keeper: Evening, shipwright. Every ship on this desk was built folded flat and sealed in its bottle. The threads through the cork raise her rigging, but a thread only runs taut when the bottle is turned just so.

      Anchor / cork diagram

      Fictional tabletop rigging model. The cork's eye is a gimballed bead that always hangs level. A thread runs taut when (1) roll brings its hull anchor to the bottom of the bottle (6 o'clock, straight below the bead), within its roll band, and (2) the neck's pitch matches the thread's lead, within its pitch band.

      Optional tilt

      Move the device if you wish. Never need to. If you enable it, tilting front-to-back rolls the bottle and lifting the right edge tips the neck up, on exactly the same detents as the buttons. Pulling is always a deliberate tap. Readings stay on this device and are never stored or sent.
