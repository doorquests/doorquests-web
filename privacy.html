Privacy Policy

DoorQuests is an individually operated interactive-entertainment and learning website. This policy describes the website's currently understood information practices, including browser-local progress and requests to outside service providers. The site includes child-friendly and school-grade experiences, so parents and guardians should also read Section 8.

1. Information you choose to provide

Visitors can access publicly available pages without registering an account. If you email knock@doorquests.com, the operator receives your email address, message and any information you choose to include. This may be used to respond, handle technical or accessibility feedback, assess permissions requests, resolve concerns and maintain necessary correspondence. Please do not include passwords, payment-card details, a child's identifying information or sensitive personal information unless strictly necessary.

2. Information stored in your browser

Individual DoorQuests experiences have different approaches to saving data. Some use browser storage, including localStorage, for choices, progress, unlocked outcomes, settings or optional saved-state features. Others work entirely in memory or provide explicit export/import options. Browser data ordinarily stays on the device where it was created, but this statement is not a guarantee that every website feature operates offline or that all third-party connections are absent. Clearing site data, using a different browser/device or updating an experience may remove or reset saved state. Consult the individual experience's controls and notices where provided.

3. Hosting, security and technical requests

The website's source is published through GitHub Pages, and domain, security or delivery services may involve Cloudflare. When browsers connect to hosting or other infrastructure providers, those providers can process technical information such as IP addresses, requested URLs, timestamps, browser/device characteristics and network/security information for delivery, operation, diagnostics and protection. Technical logging and retention depend on the providers and applicable configurations; the operator does not claim that all such information is never received or retained by them.

4. Third-party resources

Some pages load external fonts from Google Fonts or other third-party resources. The browser may contact the corresponding providers and disclose technical connection information, including IP address and requested resource. Some individual interactive experiences are deliberately self-contained and use restrictive content-security policies that block external connections. The practices vary by page. External websites reached through links are governed by their own privacy notices.

5. Cloudflare Web Analytics and advertising

Cloudflare Web Analytics is used on selected DoorQuests pages, including the main homepage, some collection and experience pages, and the Contact, Terms and Privacy pages. Its deployment is not necessarily uniform across all experiences. Analytics helps assess aggregate visits and technical performance; browser requests to Cloudflare can involve the page being visited and technical connection information. Cloudflare describes Web Analytics as not using cookies or localStorage to track visitors and not fingerprinting users for analytics. This is separate from progress stored locally by an experience. DoorQuests does not currently operate behavioral advertising or sell visitor personal information, based on the operator's current practices. The site does not implement a separate response to browser Do Not Track signals. If applicable data-rights requirements or browser preference signals require additional controls, those requirements still apply.

Provider information: Cloudflare Web Analytics · Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

6. Why information may be used or disclosed

Correspondence is used to communicate with visitors, address support or rights requests, operate the site, prevent abuse and satisfy legal obligations. Hosting, security, analytics, email and resource providers may process information needed to deliver their services. Information may be disclosed where required by law or reasonably necessary to protect rights and safety. No public visitor account database, checkout or payment-card collection feature is offered through the current website.

7. Retention and visitor choices

Browser-stored progress or preferences generally remain until removed through an experience's controls, the browser's site-data settings, or browser/device processes. Emails and related communications may be kept for as long as reasonably necessary to address the correspondence, maintain records, protect rights or satisfy applicable legal obligations. Provider logs and analytics data have provider-specific retention practices. You may contact us about access, correction, deletion or other applicable privacy rights concerning information under the operator's control; requests will be considered under applicable law and legitimate retention requirements.

8. Children, Kidortals and grade-level learning

DoorQuests contains material appealing to children and families, including Kidortals and kindergarten and elementary-grade Gradortals. These areas should not be described as categorically unrelated to children under 13. Many sampled younger-audience experiences are designed to operate locally without requiring a name or account, but different pages may use different technical resources and browser storage. Adults should supervise younger children's use as appropriate and avoid sending us a child's personal information. Parents or guardians may contact knock@doorquests.com about information they believe a child has provided, including requests to review or delete operator-held correspondence where applicable.

Whether a particular part of the website is child-directed or mixed-audience, and whether any parental notice or consent obligations apply, depends on its actual audience, features, data collection and third-party providers. This policy does not represent that all child-related regulatory requirements have been conclusively assessed or that permission has been obtained where required. See the FTC's Children's Privacy guidance.

9. Security and data accuracy

We use service providers and software features intended to support website operation, but no website or network transmission can be guaranteed perfectly secure. Please limit sensitive information in messages. If material features, processors, analytics or storage practices change, this notice should be updated to reflect actual practices.

10. Changes to this notice

The original policy took effect on October 16, 2026. This revised notice is dated November 20, 2026. Later changes will be reflected on this page with a new revision date. Earlier versions may differ as the site expands.
