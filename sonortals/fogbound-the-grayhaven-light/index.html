      The keeper’s primer

      Page 1 of 5

      The harbor

      The chart fans out from your lighthouse at the bottom. Nine bearings run from West on the left to East on the right. Close to the lamp is the coast: most bearings end on rocks; the channel (marked with buoys and its name) is the only safe way in. Gold harbors have two channels.

      Vessels come in from the fog and head straight for the coast. A vessel that reaches the coast on a channel bearing is home. On any other bearing it runs aground.

      Distance: nearer vessels are louder, clearer (less muffled) and sound more often. Direction: in stereo, left is west and right is east. With one speaker, one earbud or no sound, turn on Sight cues: each sound appears as a ripple on the chart and a caption, from the very same event.

      The beam

      Aim the beam with the bearing buttons, the ◀ ▶ buttons, the arrow keys or by tapping the chart. When the beam rests on a vessel that is within the light’s reach (the dashed brass ring), you sight her: she appears on the chart, a bright shimmer joins her sound, and her name appears under “In the beam”. The beam works the same in every mode — it is the sure way to find a bearing.

      A vessel beyond the reach can be heard but cannot see your light yet.

      Orders

      • Steady (▬): hold this bearing. Right for a vessel already on a channel bearing.
      • Steer West (• ▬) / Steer East (▬ •): turn to the nearest channel on that side, then hold it. Turning takes about 3½ seconds per bearing, and she moves in more slowly while turning.
      • Heave To (• • •): stop for a few seconds. Turning carries on. Each vessel will heave to twice at most.

      An order goes to the vessel picked under “In the beam” (the nearest, unless you pick another). She answers in her own voice: two short calls mean “understood”; one falling note means “unable”. The log tells you whether the order was sound.

      Hazards and keeping score

      • Drift (Silver, Gold): an unguided vessel may slide sideways, one bearing every 10–12 seconds. Listen to her move. Any order from you stops the drift.
      • One at a time: a channel takes a few seconds to clear after each arrival. A vessel that enters while it is in use scrapes the one ahead. Heave one of them to.
      • Gusts (Gold): a few seconds of wind muffle every sound. Nothing is hidden: captions and ripples carry on.

      Each vessel: guided home 10, home without your help 6, scraped 3, aground 0. Unsound orders cost 2, “unable” or refused orders 1, unanswered signals 1 (at most 5). The light is kept — and the tier key earned — when every vessel is home undamaged. Pausing, replaying sounds, captions and Sight Mode never change the score.
