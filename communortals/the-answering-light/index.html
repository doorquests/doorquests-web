      Ivo has acknowledged

        Ivo’s tower looks over the open sea, but a headland hides the reef from him. Your cliff overlooks the reef, but fog hides the open sea. His chart holds only what your signals have told him.

        How signs work

        This lantern language is fictional, invented for this experience. It is not a real maritime signal code.

        The eight signs

          Frame positions

          The same lanterns mean different things in a raised, lowered or doubled frame. Try it and watch how Ivo answers.

          Order

          Ivo reads lanterns left to right. If he cannot read an order, he will show you one he can.

          The beam dial

          The dial is not a word. It aims the frame’s beam: Left, Ahead or Right, always as the ferry faces the harbor. What the aim means depends on the sentence it lights.

          Keyboard

          1–8 raise signs · S, Q, U set the frame · L, A, R, 0 set the beam · Backspace lowers the last lantern · Ctrl+Enter sends (or Tab to Send). Shortcuts are ignored while you type in a text box.

          Help, one step at a time
          1. Look at who can see what. You can see the reef but not the ferry. Ivo can see the ferry but not the reef.
          2. Send the same signs in two different frames and compare Ivo’s answers. A lowered frame asks; it does not deny.
          3. Lanterns read actor, action, target. Aim the beam at a channel when you tell Ivo what you see. Ask Ivo, with a lowered frame, about what only he can see.
