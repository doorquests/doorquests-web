How to play

  1. Recognise. Each door has a shaped opening: Vertical, Horizontal, Diagonal or Chevron. The next shapes are also listed at the top right.
  2. Configure. Choose the same shape: arrow keys, W A S D, 1–4, or the shape buttons. You can change shape at any time; only the shape you hold at the moment you cross counts.
  3. Cross. Press Space (or Enter, the CROSS button, or tap the corridor) when the door reaches the golden threshold frame. The shrinking ring and the beat dots count you in. The door always lands on the beat or, in later stages, exactly halfway between beats.

Judgement

  • Wrong shape — right time, wrong shape: a miss.
  • Too early — pressing just before a door's window counts as a miss for that door (so button-mashing never pays). Presses with no door nearby are simply ignored.
  • Too late — no crossing before the window closes.
  • One press can judge only one door. A miss resets your combo.

Score

Perfect 1,000 · Great 700 · Good 400 · Miss 0, multiplied by your combo: ×1 (1–4 in a row), ×2 (5–9), ×3 (10–19), ×4 (20+). Successful crossings add echo notes to the music; longer combos add layers.

Settings & timing

Sound and motion
Timing calibration

If your crossings feel consistently early or late (Bluetooth headphones, TV screens, slow touchscreens), adjust here. Both values are saved and can be reset to 0 at any time.

Input offset: positive if you tend to press late (your presses are judged as if this many ms earlier). Audio offset: positive plays the music earlier, to cancel speaker/headphone delay.

Tap test: press Start, then press Space (or tap the circle) on each of the 8 flashing beats.

Endless mode

An unending corridor from a seed: the same seed always builds the same doors, so you can replay or share a run. Tempo rises by 2 BPM every 8 doors (100 → 160). Silver timing windows. Five sparks; a miss costs one, every 25-door combo returns one.
