    How to play
    
  
      
    
    1. Open doors. Each door hides a different microgame.
      2. 
    
    2. Follow the instruction shown when the door opens — controls are listed under it.
      3. 
    
    3. Finish before the timer runs out. The bar counts only your playing time; when it says WATCH, watch first.
      4. 
    
    4. Failure costs a life. Success builds your combo.
      5. 
    
    5. The pace accelerates. Speed rises through ten levels, then holds.
      6. 
    
    6. Survive as many doors as you can. Every 25th door you pass restores a lost life.
      7. 
  
    
  
    Controls
    
  
    Arrow keys move or choose · Space / Enter acts · number keys 1–9 pick an option · Esc pauses. Mouse and touch work everywhere: tap options, drag or tap to aim, and use the buttons under the door when they appear.
    
  
    Scoring
    
  
      
  
      Pausing is fair, not free
      
  
      Press Esc (or switch tabs / leave the window) to pause. Time stops. If a challenge was open, it is cancelled — no life lost, no points — and when you resume you face a fresh version of the same microgame. So a pause never gives extra thinking time on the challenge you saw.
      
  
      If the page freezes briefly, frame gaps longer than 0.1 s are not counted against you.
      
  
      Refreshing
      
  
      Refreshing or closing the page ends the run in progress (it is not saved). Your records from finished runs stay.
      
  
      
      


  
      Records
      
  
      Kept only in this browser. Not verified and not tamper-proof. Practice is never recorded.
