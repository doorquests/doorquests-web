      How to play

      A storm is circling the house. Keep the candle lit until dawn — five minutes of night.

      Two dangers

      Gusts. An open window facing the wind lets a draft in. Drafts travel through open doors, losing half their strength at every doorway. A strong draft reaching the candle drains its stability fast.

      Stale air. The candle slowly uses up its fresh-air reserve. Open windows bring air in; air also travels through doors (and a little under closed ones). Seal the house too tightly and the flame starves.

      Reading the wind

      Each window is marked Windward (facing the wind — dangerous when open), Angled (partly exposed) or Sheltered (safe ventilation). When the wind turns, these labels change. Forecasts warn you before the wind turns and before gusts hit.

      Controls

      Tap a door or window on the plan, or use the list under it — both do the same thing. A door or window needs half a second to swing before it can be moved again. Keyboard: Tab to a control, Enter or Space to toggle, P to pause.

      Seals and scores

      Reaching dawn in a ranked night earns that tier's seal for your Gamortal Keyring. Your score (0–100) rewards a steady flame, a calm candle room, healthy air, never going critical, and not toggling more than you need to. Practice nights never touch your records.

      A note on real fire

      This is a stylised game model. Real candles do not exhaust a room's air in minutes, and you should never manage real flames around open windows in a storm or leave one unattended.
