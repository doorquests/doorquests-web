Gamortal #026Flux Garden

Grow the defense. Protect the living heart.

In the ruins of an old conservatory grows the Heartroot, a luminous living tree. Invading creatures are coming for it. Plant seeds, water and shade them, let them grow, and raise a garden whose plants guard, heal and strengthen one another.

Keyboard

1–9, 0: choose a seed (Esc or the same key again clears it). Arrow keys or WASD: move the garden cursor. Enter or Space: plant the chosen seed at the cursor, or inspect what grows there. U: uproot the plant at the cursor (half its cost back if alive). N: start the next wave now. O: switch view (plain, water, light, ecology, ranges and route). F: speed ×1, ×2 or ×3. X: describe the garden in words. G: plant and creature guide. P: pause. Tap or click a bed to move the cursor there; tap again to plant.
