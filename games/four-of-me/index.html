Gamortal #028Four of Me

One minute. Four versions of you. One impossible heist.

You have sixty seconds inside the Meridian Vault’s security floors — and you get to live them four times. Every loop, the versions of you from earlier loops come back and do exactly what you did. Hold the door for yourself. Distract the guard for yourself. Then, in the fourth minute, walk out with the artifact.
