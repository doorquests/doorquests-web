Gamortal #027Neon Drifter

Don’t follow the road. Draw it.

A small neon car races across a city of light, and the road ends just ahead of it. You never steer: you draw the road, in real time, and the car drives whatever you build. Smooth curves are fast. Sharp ones skid. Every unit of road costs Neon Charge.
