        How to keep the light
        
  
          
    
        • You control only the beam. Ships steer themselves toward their docks — but they navigate by your light.
          • 
    
        • A lit ship gains confidence, sees where the safe channel really is and turns back onto it (it cannot turn faster than its hull allows).
          • 
    
        • An unlit ship slowly loses confidence. Its idea of where the channel lies drifts — usually the way the tide is running — and it steers by that mistaken idea, toward rocks, shoals or other ships.
          • 
    
        • Ships are lost only for real reasons: striking a reef, grounding (deep-draft ships on shoals), hitting the shore, colliding, or drifting out to sea. Never because a timer runs out.
          • 
    
        • The vessel list shows each ship’s status — ● Safe, ◆ Needs guidance, ▲ At risk, ‼ Critical — its confidence, and act within: how long it can wait for your light before it can no longer be saved. On the chart the same states appear as ring patterns.
          • 
    
        • Confident ships see each other and slow down to give way. Ships in the dark do not.
          • 
    
        • Fog drains confidence faster and softens your beam. Focused Light (hold Space or ✦) narrows the beam, reaches further and cuts through fog, but uses a reserve that refills slowly.
          • 
    
        • Working buoys help ships nearby hold their course (confidence fades half as fast). Broken buoys do not.

            Controls
            
  
            Mouse: move to aim (or drag). Touch: drag on the chart around the lighthouse, or hold ◀ ▶. Keys: ← → or A D rotate (hold Shift for fine aiming) · 1–6 follow that vessel with the beam · Space Focused Light · P or Esc pause · M sound · R restart (press twice) · S read the harbor status aloud.
            
  
            Finished nights are saved automatically. A night interrupted by a reload starts again from the beginning. Practice is never recorded.
            
  
            
            

  
            Practice
            
  
            One ship, no score, no records. A dashed line shows where the ship believes the channel is — leave it dark and watch that belief drift; light it and watch it turn back.
