        How to fly
        
  
          
    
        1. You cannot steer. The balloon drifts with the wind at its altitude. The wind chart shows every layer: its height, direction (arrow) and speed.
          2. 
    
        2. Burner (hold Space / ↑ / the ▲ button) heats the air: you slow your fall, then climb. It uses fuel. Fuel never drains while the burner is off.
          3. 
    
        3. Vent (hold ↓ / V / the ▼ button) lets hot air out: you slow your climb, then sink. Venting is free, but the heat you dump costs fuel to win back.
          4. 
    
        4. The balloon has inertia. Air cools slowly, so you keep sinking a little after you stop venting, and keep drifting a little after you change layers. Start early.
          5. 
    
        5. Ballast (B): drop a sandbag to make the balloon lighter — it then floats with less heat. An emergency lift, not a way down.
          6. 
    
        6. Land on the flagged platform gently: slow enough downward and sideways. Landing anywhere else, or too hard, ends the flight — try again.
