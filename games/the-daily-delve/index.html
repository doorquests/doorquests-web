      How to play
      
  
        
    
      1. Choose a door. Each floor has three. Read the crest and listen at the door. Your choice is final once the door opens.
        2. 
    
      2. Face what lies beyond: a fight, treasure, a trap, a merchant, a place to rest, or something stranger. From floor 4, treasure may be guarded — win the fight (or flee and lose the prize) before you choose.
        3. 
    
      3. Descend. Ten floors end at the Heart Warden. On floor 10 the three doors are three ways to face it.
        4. 
  
      
  
      Clues
      
  
      Sounds never lie — if you hear something, it tells you what is there. Sometimes you hear nothing (on floor 1 you always hear; on floor 2, nine times in ten; deeper, about three times in five). Crests are hints, not promises. Measured from the dungeon generator over many days:
      
  
      
  
      Combat
      
  
        
    
      • Before you act you see what the enemy intends — Attack, Heavy blow (×1.8) or Guard — and the exact damage range you would take.
        • 
    
      • Attack: your weapon range minus the enemy's armour (+3 armour while it guards). Defend: halve the damage you take this turn, and your next attack gains +2. Special: your weapon's technique, once per fight. Items: draughts, Fire Flasks, Smoke Vials.
        • 
    
      • Your armour reduces every hit (minimum 1). Each victory hardens you: +1 maximum health (+2 for elites).
        • 
    
      • Poison costs 1 health per combat turn and up to 2 when you descend (it never takes your last point). Rest at a spring, an Antidote or the Rootbound Talisman cures it.
        •

      The daily dungeon
      
  
      Everyone playing on the same UTC date and tier meets exactly the same dungeon: the same doors, foes, prizes, prices and dice. Your choices change your journey, never the dungeon. The first run you finish (or abandon) each day is your first attempt; replays are practice and can only raise your personal best. The date comes from your device's clock — without a server the game cannot verify it, and local records are not tamper-proof.
      
  
      Keys
      
  
      1 2 3 or ← ↑ → open the left, centre or right door. Tab moves between buttons; Enter or Space presses. Esc opens the menu. M toggles sound.
