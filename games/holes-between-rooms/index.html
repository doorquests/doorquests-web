How to play

  • Aim and putt. Press anywhere on the course and drag back, away from where you want the ball to go. The arrow shows the direction; drag farther for more power. Let go to putt. Drag back to the start (or press Esc) to cancel.
  • Doorways. A glowing doorway is linked to the doorway with the same symbol and colour. The ball keeps its speed and its direction relative to the door — like walking through: what was on your left stays on your left. A door on a north wall can open onto an east wall.
  • Furniture. If it stands up, the ball bounces off it. If it is painted on the floor (rugs, tiles, floor symbols), the ball rolls over it. Rugs and carpets slow the ball; polished floors keep it rolling.
  • The cup. The ball drops only if it reaches the middle of the cup slowly enough. Too fast and it skips across.
  • Hazards. Water costs one penalty stroke and returns the ball to where you played from. Stairs pull the ball back down.
  • Clockwork. Moving parts never stop (they pause when you pause). Their reach is marked on the floor. A ball left inside that mark is nudged clear before your next putt.
  • Stroke limit. After 8 strokes without holing out, the hole is picked up and scores 9.
