Every ship has a destination. Every harbor deserves a moment of peace.

Stillwater Harbor is small: a weathered pier, one crane, a modest warehouse, a fishing boat rocking at its mooring — and a freighter coming in through the morning mist. Welcome the ships, move their cargo well, and keep the quiet character of the bay.
