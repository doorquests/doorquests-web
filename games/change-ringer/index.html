Gamortal #030Change Ringer

Every bell has its place. Every change has its moment.

High above a sleeping village, the bells of St. Aldwyn wait in their frame. Ring them as English bell-ringers have for four hundred years — not a melody, but rows: every bell once, in order, the order changing one swap at a time until the bells come home to rounds.
