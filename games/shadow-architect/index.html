Objective

    Structure

    Keyboard

    Move: arrow keys or WASD (hold to keep walking). E or Enter: work the selected structure (open/close, raise/lower, flip, throw a lever; slides and bridges move along their rails). R: push forward / turn clockwise. Q: pull back / turn anticlockwise. Tab (Shift+Tab): select the next (previous) structure. B: Shadow Blueprint. X: describe your surroundings. Z: wait a moment. P or Esc: pause. You can also tap a structure in the view to select it.

    The Lantern Alley

    Paused

    The city is frozen: watchers, patrols, searchlights and clockwork all wait while you are paused.

    Caught!

    Returning to the last checkpoint costs 150 points. The city keeps its clocks: patrols and searchlights carry on from where they are now. You get a short moment of grace on your return.
