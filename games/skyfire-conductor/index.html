    How to play
    
  
      
    
    1. Survey the sky. Each glowing shell is a firework waiting for a spark. Its shape tells you its type; its letter is its musical note.
      2. 
    
    2. Choose an ignition point and launch. Your rocket bursts there 0.35 s later.
      3. 
    
    3. Every shell the burst touches ignites, then explodes after its short fuse — and its burst can light more shells.
      4. 
    
    4. Reach the goal with your limited launches. Unused launches are worth points if you clear the goal.
      5. 
  
    
  
    Shells
    
  
      
  
      Controls
      
  
      Mouse: aim and click to launch. Touch: tap to launch (or switch to aim-then-launch in Settings). Keyboard: arrows move the target (Shift for big steps), Q / E jump between shells, Space or Enter launches, F ends the round, R restarts it, Esc pauses, M mutes. The Shell targets list under the sky lets you aim without seeing it.
      
  
      Scoring
      
  
        
  
        Generations: your rocket's burst is generation 0; shells it lights are generation 1; shells they light are generation 2, and so on. Depth is the deepest generation in a chain; cascade width is the largest single generation.
