Gamortal #023The Midnight Noodle Cart

Every midnight brings a different appetite.

Run a small lantern-lit noodle cart on a street that only seems to exist after dark. Boil noodles, heat broth, chop and add toppings, and serve each bowl before your guests lose patience. Some come from across the street. Others come from somewhere considerably farther away. Everyone gets a bowl.
