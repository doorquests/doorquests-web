How to handle the page
  • Drag loose paper to move it; it snaps into outlines and slots.
  • Drag a label’s corner to peel it; drag along a perforation to tear it.
  • Tap a corner or crease to fold it, a dial to turn it, a button to press it.
  • Or select anything (tap it, or Tab to it and press Enter) and use the buttons that appear here.
