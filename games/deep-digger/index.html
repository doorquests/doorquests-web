The deeper you dig, the harder it is to leave.

Climb down into an old mine and dig for copper, silver and rare crystals. Your energy, lantern and air are limited, and wide tunnels can cave in. Plan your way back, because treasure is only yours if you carry it back to daylight.

Objective

    Keyboard

    Move or climb: arrow keys or WASD (hold to keep going; pressing toward rock turns you to face it). Space: dig the highlighted block (hold to keep digging forward). B: place a timber support where you stand. E: pick up. I: pack. M: map. R: eat a ration. T: fit a spare battery. L: ledger. P or Esc: pause. Enter at the surface: end the expedition.
