      How to play
      
  
        
    
      1. Launch the ball — Space or Enter (or the Launch button). Hold ↓/S (or hold Launch) to charge the plunger, release to fire. A lit rune lane hit straight from the plunger is a skill shot.
        2. 
    
      2. Keep it alive with the flippers — ← or A for the left flipper, → or D for the right. Hold a flipper up to catch (cradle) the ball, then let it roll and flip again to aim: where the ball meets the flipper decides where it goes.
        3. 
    
      3. Hit bumpers, slingshots and targets for points.
        4. 
    
      4. Enter portals — the Twin Gates swap the ball between the left orbit and the upper right; the Ascension Gate lifts it to the top of the table.
        5. 
    
      5. Unlock the Hidden Door — earn a chamber key and shoot the centre door to enter a hidden chamber. Chambers have their own flippers; the ball comes back to the table when you finish, when time runs out, or if it slips past.
        6. 
    
      6. Build the multiplier — it multiplies every point. It resets when a ball drains.
        7. 
    
      7. Survive — three balls per game. A drain soon after launch is caught by ball save.
        8. 
  
      
  
      Keys
      
  
      ← / A left flipper · → / D right flipper · Space / Enter launch · ↓ / S charge the plunger · Esc or P pause · M sound · T describe the table aloud.
