      How to survive the Hollow
      
  
        
    
      • Move with WASD or the arrows (or the pad). Hold Shift (or toggle 🤫) to move quietly: slower, but your footsteps make no sound.
        • 
    
      • Sonar (Space or ◎): a pulse flows outward along the corridors — walls stop it, so it bends around corners and never shows what lies behind solid stone. Walls glow as the wave reaches them, then fade: bright echo → fading echo → remembered outline. You always feel the cells right around you.
        • 
    
      • But sound travels further than sight. A pursuer the pulse reaches by sound may hear it — even beyond what you can see — and walk to where you were when you pulsed. It does not know where you went next.
        • 
    
      • Sonar charges recharge slowly and never run out for good. A short cooldown follows each pulse.
        • 
    
      • Echo stones (Q or ◌) fly up to five cells ahead and ring where they land — pursuers investigate the stone instead of you.
        • 
    
      • Interact (E): unlock doors with the key, wake resonator nodes. Both make noise.
        • 
    
      • Shadow alcoves (◇) hide you from sight while you stand still. A pursuer who saw you go in — or who walks in to investigate a sound you made there — will still find you.
        • 
    
      • Machinery hums: near it (the gold-dotted zone) your footsteps are silent and your pulses are half as loud. Curtains swallow sound; echoing tunnels carry it twice as far; cracked walls let sonar through and are passages only you can squeeze through. Shifting walls open and close every 8 s — your memory shows them as they were when you last saw them.
        • 
  
      
  
      Pursuers
      
  
      
  
      Each pursuer moves through the labyrinth — never through walls — and changes between Patrol → Investigate → Search → Chase → Return. The Alert panel says what is happening and why: ○ Quiet · ? Heard · … Searching · ‼ Hunted. Pursuers near you are visible; the arcs around you point toward footsteps you can hear.
