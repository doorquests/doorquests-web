Sherlockortals

The Nemesis Chronicles

Thirty-One Cases. One Hidden Enemy. Every Clue Matters.

Created by The Axiom Continuum

◈ Concept Preview

A detective campaign where every case changes the last.

Sherlockortals is designed as one of the most intellectually demanding worlds in Dwortals: a 31-case mystery campaign where you are the detective, the evidence persists, and the truth grows stranger the deeper you go.

At first, each investigation appears independent. But subtle patterns, recurring symbols and impossible coincidences reveal a darker reality: someone brilliant has been shaping events from the shadows—and studying you in return.

Premise

You are the detective.

Rather than revisiting existing Sherlock Holmes stories directly, Sherlockortals keeps the Victorian investigative atmosphere while introducing original DoorQuests lore, an original campaign structure and a distinctive hidden nemesis whose influence touches every case.

Campaign structure

31 connected cases.

Each case can stand on its own as a mystery, but the full campaign forms a long game of deduction. Earlier discoveries, overlooked details and relationship choices continue to matter as the chronicles progress toward a final confrontation.

The central twist

The nemesis investigates you too.

The deeper you look into the mastermind’s design, the more you discover that your own methods, habits and deductions are being observed. In Sherlockortals, your investigative style becomes part of the final mystery itself.

Seven-story progression

The Nemesis Chronicles

The campaign unfolds across seven arcs. Each stage reshapes how you understand the evidence, the enemy and your own place inside the game.

Arc I

The First Thread

Cases 001–005

Five apparently unrelated investigations

A disappearance, an impossible theft, a coded letter, a suspicious death and a household secret seem to belong to separate corners of the city. Each case demands sharp observation, careful interviews and classic deduction.

Story progression: The world introduces your methods, your casebook and the feeling that reason can explain everything—at least for now.

Arc II

The Hidden Pattern

Cases 006–010

Repeated symbols and connections emerge

What first seemed accidental now begins to repeat. Names resurface. A strange emblem appears in multiple places. A witness from one case becomes relevant to another. Threads begin to braid into something intentional.

Story progression: The detective starts to suspect that these cases are not merely similar—they may be curated.

Arc III

The Invisible Hand

Cases 011–015

The detective realizes someone is directing events

The pattern sharpens into design. You begin to see that a hidden intelligence has not only influenced the crimes, but anticipated how investigators, suspects and institutions would respond to them.

Story progression: The search shifts from solving incidents to identifying the unseen strategist behind them.

Arc IV

The Countermove

Cases 016–020

The nemesis begins targeting the investigator

Now the game turns personal. Testimonies become traps. Messages appear where no outsider should have reached. People close to your investigations are pressured, misled or endangered. The enemy is no longer hiding their awareness of you.

Story progression: The detective must decide what to protect, what to risk and whether relentless pursuit is worth the cost.

Arc V

The Great Deception

Cases 021–025

Previous assumptions and evidence are challenged

Cases you thought were solved become unstable. Trusted conclusions fracture. Reliable witnesses may have been manipulated—or may have manipulated you. Even your own record-keeping comes under suspicion.

Story progression: Sherlockortals tests whether your deductions are truly sound when the evidence itself seems to shift beneath you.

Arc VI

The Final Game

Cases 026–030

The mastermind’s network begins unraveling

Bit by bit, the machinery behind the chronicles starts to show. Hidden associates, safe channels, financial paths, false identities and long-buried motives come into focus. The enemy’s design is still formidable, but no longer invisible.

Story progression: The detective at last begins dismantling the network instead of merely reacting to it.

Arc VII

The Last Deduction

Case 031

Final confrontation informed by all prior cases

The final case is not solved by a single clue alone. It is solved by memory, pattern recognition, consequence and character. Every preserved thread—every relationship, deduction and overlooked detail—can shape the final confrontation with the nemesis.

Story progression: The truth is revealed only when you understand both the mastermind’s plan and the version of yourself they believed they were studying.

Persistent campaign systems

The Detective’s Casebook

The Casebook is the connective tissue of Sherlockortals. It preserves what you know, what you think you know and what may later prove to matter more than expected.

What the Casebook tracks

  • Evidence: objects, documents, statements, cipher fragments and recovered links between cases.
  • Deductions: what you concluded, what you doubted and which patterns you decided to follow.
  • Relationships: trusted allies, recurring witnesses, uncertain informants and damaged reputations.
  • Consequences: earlier decisions that alter access, trust, danger and interpretation in later cases.

What makes this world distinctive

Sherlockortals is not only about solving mysteries—it is about surviving an escalating contest of intellect. The hidden enemy is watching your habits, anticipating your logic and adapting to your style. By the end, your own methods become evidence.

This gives Sherlockortals a uniquely reflective structure: each case advances the plot, but also quietly reveals how the campaign understands you.

An original DoorQuests mystery world

Every clue matters because every clue may be watching back.

Victorian atmosphere, relentless deduction and a campaign-scale intellectual duel make Sherlockortals one of the most compelling mystery concepts in Dwortals. Thirty-one cases begin as separate puzzles—but together they form a single hidden design.

When the final case arrives, the true question may not be whether you can identify the nemesis. It may be whether you can prove you were ever one step ahead at all.
