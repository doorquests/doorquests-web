What will your civilization become?

Eight Civilization Doors

Every choice shapes a world.

Explore the foundations, people, institutions and discoveries that make a civilization possible. Each of these eight doors reveals another dimension of how societies grow, interact and change over generations.

The Civortals Principle

Your choices become their history.

Civilizations are shaped by interacting decisions—not by a single path toward inevitable triumph or collapse. Explore the effects of governance, culture, resources, knowledge, infrastructure and relationships across generations, with room for resilience, reform and renewal.
