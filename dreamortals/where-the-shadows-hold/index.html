Where the Shadows Hold

Dream physics only. In waking life, shadows hold no one up — keep to solid ground.

Progress is not saved: refreshing the page starts the dream over. This page makes no network requests.

How to play

You wake in a dream where the dark is solid and light is empty air. Reach the way on in each of five chambers.

Controls

  • Lamp hook: ◀ ▶ buttons, tap a hook ring on the floor, drag the lamp, or focus the hook slider and press ← → (Home/End for hooks 1 and 8).
  • Lamp height: Low, Mid, High buttons (when the chain allows).
  • Walk: the arrow pad, tap a marked neighbouring cell, or focus the dream floor and press arrow keys or W A S D. Arrow keys only act on the control that has focus.
  • Rearrange (chamber 4): choose an object, choose a resting place, then Place it.
  • Undo steps back one action. Reset chamber starts the room over. Nothing is ever lost for good.
  • The text view describes every cell, object and shadow; the observation ledger records what you have learned and how each crossing worked.

If a change would take the ground from under your feet, the dream refuses it and tells you why. Progress is not saved between visits.

Rules of this dream (contains discoveries)
  1. Shadow is matter: a floor cell in shadow can be walked on. Lit air holds nothing.
  2. Stone pillars are always solid.
  3. Floating objects are never floor. Only their shadows are.
  4. Shadows fall away from the lamp. Moving the lamp never moves an object.
  5. The lower the lamp, the longer the shadow. An object's shadow is the object scaled outward from the point under the lamp by H ÷ (H − z), where H is lamp height and z is how high the object floats.
  6. Shadows that overlap are one surface. They never stack, double, or rise.
  7. Two objects floating at the same height cannot share the same air.
  8. The bearer of a light casts no shadow. Set the lamp down and your own body casts one: it starts under your feet and stretches away from the lamp, longer the farther you stand from it.
