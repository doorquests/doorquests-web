The Histortals Principle

History is documented. Your journey is imagined.

Historical settings, people and events should be supported by reliable evidence. Reconstructed details should be identified as reconstructions, uncertainties acknowledged, and branching story outcomes presented as fiction rather than changes to the historical record.
