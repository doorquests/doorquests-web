Step Into the Recorded Past

History is more than a date on a page.

Explore documented history through the civilizations, historical figures and defining events that shaped our world. Walk through ancient cities, investigate historical evidence, and encounter pivotal choices through carefully framed interactive stories.

Historical facts remain distinct from fictional outcomes.

Ways to Explore

Different paths. One commitment to evidence.

The Histortals Principle

History is documented. Your journey is imagined.

Historical settings, people and events should be supported by reliable evidence. Reconstructed details should be identified as reconstructions, uncertainties acknowledged, and branching story outcomes presented as fiction rather than changes to the historical record.
