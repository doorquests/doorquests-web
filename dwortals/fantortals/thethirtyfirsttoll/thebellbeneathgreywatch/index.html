The Bell Beneath Greywatch

The garrison is getting ready for an attack that no lookout can see. Beneath the courtyard, a bell that was buried after an old siege is tolling again, and every toll opens new cracks. You are no chosen champion, just someone willing to take a dangerous evening shift. Before the gate closes you must decide: evacuate, or defend?

    Hero’s Keyring

    How to play

    The basics

    Read each scene, then choose. Every choice shows what it costs before you commit: marks of the gate clock, Vitality, or items. Outcomes you haven’t discovered yet are never shown in advance. Reading, this help, the Keyring and the settings never cost time.

    The gate clock

    The main gate closes after 12 marks. The clock is not real time: it only moves when you take an action that costs marks. A false toll sounds from below every second mark. During the Warning Hour each toll widens the courtyard cracks, and when three have built up the courtyard collapses. Ringing the great bell adds one more. If the gate closes before you are done, the chapter goes on: there are other ways out.

    The bell and the map

    • Ring warns the Upper Ward and the courtyard. It shakes the cracks, which makes courtyard routes risky and brings the collapse closer. Diggers below hear the call and can learn it.
    • Silence warns no one. In the quiet you can follow the false toll to the old well, which opens a way down. Dark, unwarned lanes draw looters.
    • Reroute drops the counterweight onto the buried bell’s line. That warns the Well Quarter through old speaking pipes and opens the counterweight shaft. The deep tone stirs tollbugs below.

    Warnings you have delivered stay delivered, even if you change the bell later. So does anything the diggers have already heard. The map shows routes as open (the number is the cost in marks), risky (!) or blocked (×). A text list of every route sits under the map.

    Your Keyring

    You have 4 carry slots. Travel clothes are worn and take no slot, and up to 3 bandages share one slot. Vitality starts at 8, which is also the maximum. Knowledge (what you have learned) is kept separately from evidence (objects you carry): a rubbing can be lost while the pattern you studied stays in your head. Hesta’s trust and your promises are tracked separately too.

    Danger

    Injuries are real and healing is limited. If Vitality reaches 0 you go down, and the chapter continues as a documented setback. Nothing is secretly reset.

    Saves

    Your run saves automatically in this browser after every choice, and Epic Journey and Free Exploration are saved separately. There is no cloud or cross-device sync. Use Keyring → Saves to export or import a JSON copy. If the same run is open in two tabs, the game pauses saving and asks which copy to keep rather than overwriting the newer one.

    Settings


    There is no sound in this build. Every cue is shown as text.
