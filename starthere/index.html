The story behind the doors

It began with a question.

What if every door opened to a different experience?

Created by The Axiom Continuum, DoorQuests began as an experiment in interactive storytelling and grew into a universe of imagination, discovery, learning and exploration.

The original four collections invite you to choose your path through stories, investigate mysteries, overcome challenges and play games where decisions matter. Beyond them, Universortals opens new possibilities: explore history, science, distant worlds and the art of learning through doing.

Some doors are ready to open. Others introduce ideas still taking shape. You are free to explore at your own pace.

A new word, a simple idea

What is an Ortal?

An Ortal is an individual interactive experience behind a door. A collection brings related experiences together; a wing helps you find the kind of experience you enjoy. You do not need to memorize the names to play.

Open a door, read the instructions and make your choices. Some experiences have multiple endings, others ask you to uncover a truth or master a challenge. Each has its own rules.

Understanding the five gateways

Your journey, your choice

The Ortal Way

  1. Choose your path. Follow your curiosity and choose the kind of experience you want to enjoy.
  2. Enter and participate. Read, investigate, experiment or play. Your actions are part of the experience.
  3. Discover the outcome. Learn from the consequences, uncover an ending or earn an achievement where available.
  4. Return whenever you wish. Try another route, revisit a favorite or open an entirely different door.

Meet Wortal

Wortal is the Warden of the Doors, a friendly guide who will welcome you on the Discover page when you are ready to begin. You can choose a pathway or let chance select an experience.

Carry your discoveries

Your Keyring

Some experiences award keys, seals or other achievements as you explore. Where supported, your progress is saved in your browser rather than in a required online account.

Progress may not follow you to another device or browser, and clearing browser data can erase it. If an experience offers export and import, use those controls to preserve your progress. Rewards and saving features vary by experience.

What matters to us

Curiosity comes first.

The playable experiences linked from this introduction are free to explore. No mandatory accounts, no forced tutorial and no required purchase. DoorQuests is built around freedom of choice, imagination and discovery through participation.
