An entirely different kind of door

Wonder how? Find your way.

Not every adventure takes place in another world. Sometimes the next door opens on a question from everyday life: how to fix something, learn a new skill, solve a problem, or discover where you came from.

Howortals is the practical-learning wing of Universortals. This growing directory brings together quick explanations, hands-on skills and deeper guided journeys. The goal is simple: start with a question and leave knowing what to do next.

Family & History · Flagship concept

Geneortals — Finding Your Past

Every family has a story. Begin with what you already know, trace documents and memories, distinguish evidence from assumptions, and preserve what you discover. Geneortals is envisioned as a deeper, ongoing Howortals journey.

A growing library, not a fixed 31-door limit

These are category and guide previews, not completed interactive tutorials. Quick how-to answers may take minutes; hands-on lessons may create something useful; long-form journeys may unfold over time. Future guides should be accessible, accurately sourced, reviewed for safety and kept current when technology or procedures change.

Health guidance is educational, not individual medical advice. Safety-sensitive repairs require clear precautions, and genealogy should respect the privacy of living people.
