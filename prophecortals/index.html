Every Prediction Has a Story

What did people believe would happen?

Explore humanity's enduring fascination with predicting what comes next. Encounter ancient oracles, historical prophecies, forgotten forecasts, mysterious warnings and extraordinary predictions. Through classic stories, modern twists and interactive mysteries, investigate what people believed would happen, what actually happened, and whether the future can ever truly be known.

Eight paths into the mystery of tomorrow. Beliefs, historical claims, and scientific forecasts are explored on their own terms, with documented evidence distinguished from legend and imaginative storytelling.

The Prophecortals Principle

Follow the words. Examine the evidence.

Explore belief traditions with care and respect. Distinguish authentic dated sources from later attributions, symbolic interpretations from specific testable predictions, and scientific forecasts from claims of supernatural foresight. Where records are incomplete or disputed, uncertainty is part of the investigation.
