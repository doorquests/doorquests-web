31 Adventures into the Unknown

Magical Lands. Living Legends. Endless Possibilities.

Created by The Axiom Continuum

The magic begins with a choice

What waits beyond your next door?

A mysterious threshold leads to a world fractured into magical dominions. Wander among living forests and flooded archives, face creatures of fire and shadow, discover spells, and uncover artifacts that whisper of a forgotten past.

You are the traveler. Across 31 connected choose-your-path adventures, the allies you earn, relics you claim, powers you learn, and promises you keep may shape the fate of every realm. The unknown is waiting—and the ending has not been written.

The Magiortals Creative Principle

Magic is discovered through choices.

Every realm has its own nature. Every creature has a reason to act. Every spell, relic, and alliance can change what happens next. Magiortals is designed as one evolving fantasy journey—not 31 isolated encounters. Your Hero’s Keyring is envisioned to preserve the discoveries and consequences that make your path your own.

The last threshold is still unwritten

Will you change the world—or let it change you?

These are story concept previews, not yet playable adventures. One day, the doors will open onto choices only you can make. Until then, explore the possibilities and imagine which path you would take.
