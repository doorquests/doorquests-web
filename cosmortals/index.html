The Cosmortals Principle

Wonder begins with curiosity. Discovery depends on evidence.

Cosmortals celebrates the imagination without confusing established observations, working scientific hypotheses, and speculative possibilities. Each journey can be imaginative while remaining clear about what science has—and has not—confirmed.
