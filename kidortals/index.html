Welcome, little explorers

A universe made for childhood

Kidortals is a special wing of DoorQuests created for children and the families who share their adventures. Here, timeless fables, beloved fairy tales, worldwide folklore and extraordinary original stories come to life through choices, exploration and play.

Race alongside the tortoise and the hare. Help a lost animal find its way home. Discover a castle beyond the clouds. Solve a mystery, invent an ending, or step inside a story your grandparents once heard as children.

Our guiding principle: Teach through storytelling, exploration, and play.

Beyond the clouds, Marigold the Storybook Keeper watches over a magical library. Its oldest storybook is losing its pages, and ten extraordinary worlds need your help.

Adventures for every little explorer

Find the right door for you

Rather than organizing adventures by school grade, Kidortals will offer developmental groupings that support different interests, abilities, and reading levels. Ages are guidance, not requirements.

Ages 3–5

Little Explorers

Read-aloud narration, large colorful buttons, simple choices, sounds, and visual discovery. Designed for exploring alongside a parent or independently where appropriate.

Ages 6–8

Young Adventurers

Branching stories, simple puzzles, moral choices, reading development, and multiple endings.

Ages 9–12

Curious Questers

Deeper mysteries, strategic decisions, longer adventures, folklore, and more complex puzzles.

All ages

Family Adventures

Cooperative storytelling, shared decisions, family game nights, and experiences that parents and grandparents can enjoy with children.

For children, parents & grandparents

The Kidortals design philosophy

We believe childhood should be filled with wonder, imagination, curiosity, and meaningful play. These principles guide the development of every Kidortals adventure.

  • ✦
    Teach without preaching.

    Let children discover lessons through the consequences of their choices.

  • ✦
    Encourage curiosity rather than punish mistakes.

    A wrong choice can lead to another adventure.

  • ✦
    Preserve the magic of childhood.

    Not everything needs to be an academic exercise.

  • ✦
    Encourage creativity.

    Some adventures could allow children to create their own endings.

  • ✦
    Protect children by design.

    No advertising, unnecessary personal data collection, strangers contacting children, unmoderated chat, manipulative purchases, or addictive reward mechanisms.

  • ✦
    Respect cultural storytelling traditions.

    Preserve meaningful context rather than treating every traditional story as interchangeable fantasy.

  • ✦
    Encourage family participation.

    Give parents and grandparents opportunities to share stories they grew up with.

  • ✦
    Make accessibility foundational.

    Optional narration, readable text, adjustable sound, large controls, and untimed alternatives are development goals.

These are design commitments for future development, not a certification of experiences that have yet to be built. This landing page contains no forms, advertising, analytics scripts, chat, or third-party embedded content. Before child-directed interactive experiences are published, their privacy, accessibility, safety, and age-appropriateness should be independently reviewed, including any site-wide infrastructure.
