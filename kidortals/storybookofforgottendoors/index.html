Settings

How are we playing?

Change any time. A new choice takes effect when you press Done. Every page and finished story door stays safe; if you are in the middle of a story door, that door starts fresh. Nothing about you is saved.

Sound and reading
50%

One volume for both reading aloud and gentle sounds. Reading aloud can be used even when gentle sounds are off. At 0% everything is silent.

Comfort
For grown-ups

Progress is kept only in this browser on this device, under one key for this game. It saves at the end of each story door; a door left halfway starts fresh next time, and finished doors are always kept. There are no accounts, chats, ads or purchases.

Start over?

This clears every recovered page and choice in this storybook on this device. Other DoorQuests games are not touched. You can't undo this.
