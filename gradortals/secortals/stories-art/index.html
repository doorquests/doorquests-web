The Very Literal Players

Prototype · not published · v1.0.1

Choose Your Path. Find the Truth. Unlock the Impossible.

Behind a door no bigger than a postage stamp

sits the Matchbox Theatre. Its thumb-sized actors perform exactly what the script says, and nothing more. You are the director. Choose strips, put them in order, and rehearse. When something happens that the script never set up, the players stumble, and you get to fix it.

There are no scores, no timers and no right story. Continuity is checked. Your ideas, reasons, tone, ending and title are never graded.

Your playbill

For grown-ups

Everything runs on this device. Nothing is uploaded or shared. Progress for each production is saved in this browser only, if saving is allowed. Read-aloud uses the device’s own voice when available, and never starts by itself.

Stumbles are objective continuity checks: something the script asked for was not set up yet. “Optional ideas” are craft suggestions and never block a draft. The strip catalog is small, so the theatre can only check stories built from its strips.
