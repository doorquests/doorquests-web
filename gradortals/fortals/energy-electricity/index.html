The weir model

Word helper
  • Energy is what makes things move, warm up, light up or make sound. It moves from place to place, but it is never destroyed.
  • Current is charge flowing around the wire. It is not energy, so it is never added to the ledger.
  • Transfer means energy moving from one thing to another, like from the lamp into the air.
  • Waste means energy that goes somewhere we didn't want for the job. It is still real energy.
