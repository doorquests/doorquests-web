A journey of learning awaits

Discover something new at every grade

From first letters and numbers to advanced science, writing, mathematics and life skills, Gradortals will bring K–12 subjects to life through age-appropriate interactive discoveries and thoughtful challenges.

Kindortals, Firsortals, Secortals, Thirdortals & Fortals · Twenty-Five Flagship Experiences Available

All 78 K–12 subject collections have their Top 31 experience concepts selected and Founder-approved—2,418 concepts in total. The six Kindergarten, six First Grade, six Second Grade and six Third Grade flagships, plus Fourth Grade Literature & Writing #001, are available to play; the other five Fourth Grade categories and Grades 5–12 remain in development. Concept approval and independent technical review are not the same as final Founder gameplay acceptance.
