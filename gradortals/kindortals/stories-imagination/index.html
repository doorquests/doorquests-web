The way to Lantern Hill

For grown-ups

What this teaches: an early choice can matter much later in a story. The child packs three of seven things before the journey begins. Each thing used along the way stays where it was used, and the story remembers it. Later places, helpers and the homecoming change because of those earlier choices.

No best answer: all 35 ways to pack the suitcase work, and every path brings Glim safely home. Different choices make different stories, not better or worse ones. There are no scores, timers or wrong answers.

Try asking: "Why did the ducks come to the party?" or "What would happen if you packed something else?"

The ending: the suitcase opens into a wordless pop-up book of the child's own journey. The "Read my story" button shows the same story in words, built from what actually happened.

Reading aloud: "Read to me" uses your device's built-in voice, if it has one. Voices differ between devices, and some devices have none. No recorded human narration is included.

Privacy: no accounts, no personal details, no tracking and no internet connection. Progress is saved only in this browser on this device.
