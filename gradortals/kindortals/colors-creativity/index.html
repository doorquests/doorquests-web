    For grown-ups

    The idea: an unplanned mark can start an original idea. Dab the paint pot drops a splat, and your child decides what it could become.

    How to play

    • Turn the splat with the curved arrows (or the arrow keys when the picture is focused).
    • Tap picture pieces — wheels, wings, a sail, legs, windows, eyes — to add them. Tap again to take one off.
    • "New splat" swaps the splat but keeps the chosen pieces. "Wake it up!" brings the creation to life. "Change it" goes back to editing.
    • After three pages, all three creations live together in the Awakening World.

    No single right answer

    Every combination of pieces makes something: a flying house, a sailing creature, a splat that simply sits and smiles. Dab only describes what happened and never judges. There are no scores, timers or wrong answers.

    Playing again

    "New splats!" starts again with different splat shapes, colors and piece choices. Progress saves on this device only, so a refresh picks up where you were.

    Sound and voice

    Chimes are made by the browser and need no download. Voice uses your device's built-in speech, if it has one; voices differ between devices and some devices have none. Every spoken line is also shown in Dab's speech bubble.

    Privacy

    No account, no personal information, no ads, no tracking and no internet connection are used.

    Start over?

    This clears the creations saved for this activity on this device. Nothing else is changed.
