Grown-ups: how will the first sounds be given?

For grown-ups: this prototype has no recorded beginning-sound audio, so the game cannot play the first sounds itself. Choose “A grown-up says the sounds” to read each sound from the on-screen script. “Picture practice” is a looking activity, not a listening one: no beginning sounds are spoken, though optional chimes and the whole-word voice may still play. The device voice only says whole words, and not every device has one.
