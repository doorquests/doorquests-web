Three Knocks at Sorrel Pass

Choose Your Path. Find the Truth. Unlock the Impossible.

A blizzard has closed Sorrel Pass. One stone refuge stands at the top, and its door is shut.

You are Ilsa Marr, the new relief warden, climbing up to take over the refuge. A letter went ahead of you. You have four watches to get someone to open the door.

About 25–35 minutes. No timers, no sound needed, no account. Every sound is written out.

How it works

The night is split into watches. You choose a few things each watch, then knock to end it. Your choices are written into the night and cannot be changed afterwards, though you can undo within a watch.

The game is told in parts. You will play more than one of them. Nothing you do later changes what has already happened.

Hints are available in each part. Nothing is timed, and nothing ends badly for anyone.

The Warden’s Drill — play any of the twelve nights from inside

Contains spoilers about the second part. Pick an outside history by its code and play the inside directly. Drill nights are practice; nothing is recorded unless you choose to save.
