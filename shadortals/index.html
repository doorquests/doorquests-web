Shadortals

The Shadow World

You didn't believe they existed. Until you saw them with your own eyes.

Created by The Axiom Continuum

Thirty-one adventures · One unfolding nightmare

The first shadow moved without its owner.

Then another appeared in your window. Another waited inside an old photograph. Nobody believed you—until the entire neighborhood's shadows turned toward something that should not exist.

In Shadortals, you begin as an ordinary person and follow one continuous choose-your-path mystery across five acts. The deeper you investigate, the more the shadows become aware of you—and the more you discover about the lives beyond the light.

Explore the campaign below. Each door offers a story preview and a glimpse of the choices that may one day lie behind it. These 31 chapters are in development, not playable releases.

A future campaign mechanic

Awareness changes everything.

The more you learn about the shadows, the more aware they become of you. Awareness may reveal hidden truths, expose dangerous thresholds, or draw the attention of beings you do not yet understand. It is not simply a score to maximize.

Across 31 proposed adventures, a future Hero's Keyring is intended to preserve your evidence, promises, allies, faction relationships, memories, and the evolving bond with your own shadow. These are planned narrative systems—not implemented functionality in this concept directory.

Every shadow has a story

The last light has not yet gone out.

The five acts build toward decisions about whether two worlds should remain separate, learn to coexist, or attempt something entirely new. For now, the doors offer a glimpse into the journey ahead.
