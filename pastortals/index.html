Approximate geological coverage. Door 08 illustrates the Quaternary scale; its prehistoric experiences would end at different times in different regions. Bars intentionally show unequal durations.

The first three doors represent most of Earth's history. Smithsonian human-origins research describes stone tools dating back at least 3.3 million years, before the Quaternary began, which is why early hominins belong in Door 07 as well as later human development in Door 08.

The Pastortals Principle

Discover Earth's story through evidence.

Scientific findings, hypotheses and reconstructed scenes must remain clearly distinguished. These are science-informed future experience concepts, not established fictional alternatives to Earth’s history. The beginning of recorded history differs by region.
