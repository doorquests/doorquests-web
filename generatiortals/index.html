The Human Timeline

What if you could live a generation's defining choices?

Some doors open to the crackle of an old radio. Others reveal an arcade, a forgotten website, or a distant world that has never seen Earth. Every generation inherits a different world—and leaves one for the next.

Here, you don't merely read about the past or predict the future. You step into someone else's moment and decide what happens next. Explore ten generational doors and six adventure formats below. Every featured story is a concept preview, not yet a playable experience.

Six Ways to Step Into Another Era

The Six Experience Types

Generatiortals is more than a list of dates. These six story formats open doors to daily life, turning points, technology, family relationships, cultural treasures, and possible futures.

EXPERIENCE TYPE 01

Growing Up Generational

Experience childhood, adolescence, school, entertainment, and family life in different periods.

Example: Your First Day in 1985

EXPERIENCE TYPE 02

Defining Moments

Experience major events through the eyes of ordinary people living through them.

Example: The Day the Wall Came Down

EXPERIENCE TYPE 03

Technology Transitions

Learn how communication, work, transportation, and entertainment changed through playable challenges.

Example: The Last Telephone Operator

EXPERIENCE TYPE 04

Generational Encounters

Navigate misunderstandings, competing priorities, and shared challenges between generations.

Example: Three Generations, One Decision

EXPERIENCE TYPE 05

Cultural Time Capsules

Discover music, toys, fashions, inventions, entertainment, and everyday objects that defined particular eras.

Example: The Box Marked 1977

EXPERIENCE TYPE 06

Generational Futures

Explore how decisions made today could influence children, grandchildren, and future civilizations.

Example: The Promise We Made to 2075

The Generatiortals Creative Principle

History is lived one choice at a time.

Different generations encounter different tools, risks, traditions, and opportunities. Generatiortals explores those experiences through believable characters, meaningful decisions, and curiosity—not stereotypes. Historical adventures distinguish real events from fictional plots; future worlds are imaginative possibilities, not predictions.

Every Era Has a Door

Which generation's world will you enter?

Follow a lost letter, decode a radio signal, save an arcade, or look back at Earth from the stars. The next story may be set long before your time—or far beyond it.
