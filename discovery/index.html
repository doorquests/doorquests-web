Every discovery begins with evidence.

A storm has scattered a museum storeroom’s labels. Examine each illustrated object, inspect sourced historical comparisons, and build defensible catalogue cards. Uncertainty matters: overstating what the evidence proves can send a card back.

Biortal · No. 001 · Intertidal ecology

The Line on the Rock

Twice a day the sea leaves. Find out who stays.

Investigate the changing bands of life on a fictional rocky shore. Place quadrats, compare competing species, test predator-exclusion cages and transplant experiments, and discover why living communities vary. Twenty investigations, three levels of difficulty, and an expanding Biortals Keyring.
