Holidortals

The Holiday Realm

Every Celebration Holds a Secret.

Created by The Axiom Continuum

Eight celebrations. Forty-eight ways to discover wonder.

Every season has its own kind of magic.

Behind these doors, the holidays are more than dates on a calendar. Visit Santa's bustling workshop, follow the Easter Bunny through secret gardens, meet Jack Frost in a snowy kingdom, and discover what happens when Cupid's best intentions go delightfully wrong.

Explore eight distinct holiday realms spanning the seasons, heartfelt milestones, light-filled festivities, and celebrations across the world. Each realm introduces six carefully organized sections filled with imaginative possibilities and the traditions that inspire them.

Discover the holiday concepts below. The doors remain closed while their seasonal glow brightens on hover. These are concept previews, not yet playable experiences.

Traditions become discoveries

Step inside the celebration.

From an enchanting snowbound mission to a lantern-lit festival, Holidortals invites visitors to discover through doing: investigate mysteries, help communities, explore enchanted places, and make choices that matter.

Fantasy holiday characters and magical kingdoms are original imaginative adventures. Real cultural and religious observances will be explored respectfully, with visitors participating as guests, neighbors, or helpers while preserving each tradition's actual meaning.

The eight realms and 48 sections form a concept directory. Specific playable experiences will be developed and linked only when ready.

There is always something worth celebrating

Which holiday will open your imagination?

Every season brings a new story. Every celebration holds a secret. And the next extraordinary moment might be waiting behind a familiar door.
