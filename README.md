## About this repository

`doorquests/doorquests-web` contains the public website and browser-based interactive experiences for **DoorQuests**, an independently operated creative entertainment and learning project. Its experiences can involve storytelling, investigation, problem-solving, simulated decisions, practical exploration, and original fictional worlds.

The website began with four classic collections:

- **Doortals** — interactive stories and branching adventures.
- **Cluortals** — mysteries, cases, and deductive investigations.
- **Chaortals** — interactive challenges and puzzles.
- **Gamortals** — playable games and game-like experiences.

DoorQuests now extends beyond those original doors:

- **Universortals** — a gateway to additional established collections, including the Possibortals, Techortals, Discortals, Dwortals, and other destinations presented on the live site.
- **Developortals** — developing and exploratory collections, including learning, career, childhood, thematic, and other future-facing experiences.
- **Possibortals** — an expanded family of experimental interactive experiences, with Original Possibortals and the named **Boundless**, **Resonance**, **Genesis**, and **Enigma** wings.

**Availability varies by destination.** A collection page may include playable experiences, work in development, and conceptual previews. A listed experience is not necessarily published, independently playtested, or finally accepted.

## Repository structure

- `index.html` — main DoorQuests landing page.
- `universortals/` and `developortals/` — gateways for expanded and developing collections.
- `possibortals/` — Possibortals directory; individual experiences may live in their own top-level collection folders.
- `gradortals/`, `kidortals/`, and other collection folders — collection pages and individual experiences.
- `assets/` — website imagery and supporting assets where present.
- `contact.html`, `terms.html`, `privacy.html` — public contact and policy pages.
- `COPYRIGHT.md` — repository rights, trademark, and content-use statement.

This is an orientation guide, not an exhaustive list of routes or a declaration that every feature is complete.

## Development and availability

The public website includes independent browser experiences with differing designs and capabilities. Some save optional settings or progress locally in the browser; others do not retain progress or have different persistence controls. Source publication, deployment, independent technical review, actual gameplay playtesting, and Founder approval are **distinct milestones**. Do not infer gameplay certification or final acceptance merely from a live link.
