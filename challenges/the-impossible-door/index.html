The Impossible Door

UNLOCK

The record of your moves

    An endless, darkened hall. Thirty doors stand in a vast circle, each remembering a world that came before. At the centre stands a final door, larger than the rest, and above it the words: THIRTY KEYS. ONE FINAL THRESHOLD. You approach. The door seems sealed. A voice speaks: “Thirty doors came before me. Some have opened them all. Others have only just arrived.” A pause. “Both may pass.”

    Keybearer insights

    The Chamber of Thirty Echoes

    Thirty panels line the chamber, one for each door that came before. Six of them speak to the wheel: their stones stand in its sockets, and their clues are written out in full above. The other twenty-four only remember; you need nothing from them, and nothing from the doors themselves.
