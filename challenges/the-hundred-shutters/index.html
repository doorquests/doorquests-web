The caretaker's desk

Morning breaks over the canal. The houses stand in a long row, their shutters closed against the pale dawn. An elderly caretaker waits beside the first house with a ledger, a ring of keys and a lantern. “Each morning I make my rounds,” he says. “Every passage changes something. But some shutters remember how many times I have passed.” Why visit the same street so many times? He smiles. “Count the passages. Then you will understand.”

    A note on Amsterdam's canal houses, and what is fiction here

    The canal ring. Amsterdam's ring of canals was laid out as a new port city at the end of the sixteenth and the beginning of the seventeenth century. Swampland was drained with canals in concentric arcs, and the city's fortified boundary was moved out to the Singelgracht; building of the north-western part of the ring, along the Herengracht, Keizersgracht and Prinsengracht, began in 1613. The narrow, gabled canal houses that line it are real: UNESCO inscribed the “Seventeenth-Century Canal Ring Area of Amsterdam inside the Singelgracht” on the World Heritage List in 2010, as a model of large-scale town planning.

    Façades. The houses in the scenes borrow from the period's façades: brick fronts, tall windows, and gables of several shapes. Stepped gables were in fashion in the first half of the century, neck gables from about the 1640s, and bell gables later in the century; the game's houses mix them freely and do not depict real buildings.

    What is fiction. The caretaker, his rounds, the hundred numbered shutters and the examinations are invented. The puzzle itself is a well-known modern recreational problem (often told with lockers or doors); it is not from seventeenth-century Amsterdam, and the setting is only the game's backdrop, not evidence of where the puzzle began.

    Sources: UNESCO World Heritage Centre, “Seventeenth-Century Canal Ring Area of Amsterdam inside the Singelgracht”; the reference article “Canals of Amsterdam”; Buffalo Architecture and History, “Amsterdam gables”. Full addresses are in the game's documentation.
