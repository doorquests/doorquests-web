      A historical note: Fresnel lenses and this puzzle

      Augustin-Jean Fresnel's lens replaced one heavy curved glass with rings of prisms set in a brass frame, bending a lamp's light into a single strong beam. It was first installed in the Cordouan lighthouse in 1823 and spread to lighthouses around the world through the nineteenth century. Keepers cleaned and tended these lenses with great care.

      The stackable rings, the mechanical cradles and their transfer rules in this game are invented puzzle apparatus. Keepers did not assemble lenses this way. The puzzle itself is a mathematical recreation, the "Tower of Hanoi", published by Édouard Lucas in 1883 and reimagined here. Carrick Ness Light and its keeper are fiction.
