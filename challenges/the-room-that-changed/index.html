The Observer's Notebook Optional · free to use · never checks your work

Hotel Belvédère · Room 312

Write what you see while you study; you can't copy the room's inventory into it. Each observation has its own page. Playing again keeps the page; clear it here. “Reset progress” erases every page.

Hints

Hints teach observation. Each costs 12 points, never the key.
How observation works here
  • Moved: the same object is somewhere new. Its old place may now be empty, but that isn't a separate change.
  • Replaced: a different object stands in the same place.
  • Turned: the same object, in the same place, now faces the other way.
  • Removed: a place is now empty and the object is gone from every room.
  • Nothing else changes: not the furniture, the light, the rain or the colours. There are no tricks.
  • Second look: once you've left the room you can look at the original again, side by side with the room as it is now, with written inventories of both. In Bronze and Silver it costs no points. In Gold it costs 8 points per look. Any second look means the run can't earn Observation Mastery. You're always told before you look.
  • Take as long as you like. Nothing is timed.
