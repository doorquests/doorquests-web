House Rules at the Lantern Inn

The rules are the puzzle.

      How to play

      You are the night clerk of the Lantern Inn. Six travelers need six rooms, and the innkeeper's posted house rules decide who may sleep where.

      • Assign rooms with the drop-down list in each room. Use Tab and the arrow keys, or tap.
      • Each rule card shows ✓ Satisfied, ✗ Broken or … Waiting as you place travelers. Press Check the rooms to have the ledger judge the whole arrangement.
      • When something seems impossible, you can investigate: the ledger can test every possible arrangement and any group of rules you choose.
      • Hints are always available and never block your progress. The final ledger notes how many you used, nothing more.

      Words the inn uses

      • Arrangement: one traveler in every room, nobody twice. There are 720 of them.
      • Satisfiable: at least one arrangement obeys every rule in the group.
      • Smallest conflict: a group of rules that is impossible together, but becomes possible if you remove any one of them.

      Saving and restarting

      Nothing is saved. Refreshing the page restarts the story. Restart story resets only this fictional inn: rooms, rules, proofs and amendment.

      Restart the story?

      This returns you to your arrival at the inn. Every room assignment, the midnight notice, your proof and any amendment will be cleared. No files or browser data are affected.
