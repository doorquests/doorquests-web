The Paradoxortals Principle

Declare the rules. Then test the consequences.

Every future experience should distinguish logical consistency, scientific plausibility and the invented rules of its fictional universe. Backward time travel and branching timelines have not been demonstrated as real phenomena; speculative models and storytelling devices should not be presented as established science.
