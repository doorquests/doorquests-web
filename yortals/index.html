A Hall of Years

One year. One door. One world at a time.

Imagine a hall of doors, each opening into a different year. Discover the events, people, inventions, music, media, science, sports and everyday lives that shaped that year.

Our first eight year doors offer a curated journey from 1968 through 2026. These are development concepts, not playable experiences.

The Yortals Approach

A historical record, not a rewritten past.

Documented events remain distinct from imagined scenes and choices. Each time capsule can explore world events, science and technology, music, film and television, sports, and everyday life. The 2026 capsule will remain explicitly unfinished while the year unfolds.
