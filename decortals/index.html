The Decortals Approach

Remember the era. Explore its world.

Future experiences may use interactive fiction, cultural exploration, historical investigation and everyday-life reconstructions. Documented events remain distinct from fictional choices, and period reconstructions should be identified as such. The 2020s is an unfolding decade, not a completed historical record.
